Here is my Alarielle, the Everqueen
I spent a long time on this model, utilizing colorshift paint and a highly customized base. I pulled bits from Sylvaneth kits as well as Commander Farsight from 40k. The rocky foundation is heavily primed cork board, the fish were sourced from Amazon, and the water is clear resin tinted with acrylic paint. My personal favorite detail is the dew droplets on the plants across the beetle's back.

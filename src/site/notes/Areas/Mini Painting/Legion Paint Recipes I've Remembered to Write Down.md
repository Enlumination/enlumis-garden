## Aquas

- Blue - Caribbean ocean x2 speed paint medium x3
- Grey - Celestra grey no shade 
- Metallic: 1 drop silver 3 drops steel
- Black: German grey, oblivion black wash, cold corpse blue light layers 
- Eyes: spiritstone red 

## B1's

- Desert yellow base coat
- 1:1 skeleton horde contrast diluted with contrast medium
- drybrush skeleton bone

> [!NOTE] B2's
> So many of these to do, an easy recipe was in order. 
- Vallejo metal color Steel base coat with airbrush
- German grey for black 
- Oblivion black wash for black
- Oblivion black wash diluted 2:1 with medium for steel
- Dry brush steel over entire model VERY LIGHTLY 
- Thin wash later for circles on shoulders after dry brush 
- Sanguine scarlet for leader belly 
- Bold pyrrole red for highlight there 
- Vallejo dark blue for blue belly
- Adriatic Blue for highlight 
- Spiritstone red for red dot 
- Soulstone blue for blue dot 
- GWS fluorescent red and blue on dots, highlight **dot**
